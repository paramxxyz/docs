---
title: "Account events"
description: "Orders, fills, positions, cash and settlements on the account channel, as they happen."
---

The `account` channel pushes what happens to an account, in order, as it happens. Accounts are public onchain and through the REST routes, so subscribing needs no login.

```json
{"op": "subscribe", "channel": "account", "addresses": ["0x4ba4…"]}
```

Every event has `channel: "account"`, `type`, `address`, `id` (unique; use it to drop duplicates) and `ts` (epoch ms), plus:

| `type` | Fields | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `order` | `status`, `intent_id`, `order_id`, `client_order_id`, `side`, `contract_id`, `price`, `total_amount`, `filled_amount`, `resting_amount` | an order placed through the API: `resting`, `filled` or `partially_filled` when placed; `cancelled`; `expired` (up to a minute after its expiry) |
| `fill` | `trade_id`, `intent_id`, `client_order_id`, `contract_id`, `side`, `role` (`maker` / `taker`), `price`, `amount`, `total_price`, `tx_digest` | each trade the chain executes, once for each side |
| `position` | `contract_id`, `amount` (signed; negative is short) | after each fill |
| `balance` | `cash` | after fills, deposits, withdrawals and settlements |
| `settlement` | `contract_id`, `outcome` (`yes` / `no`), `amount`, `payout` (the cash credited) | when a tile the account held settles |

```json
{"channel": "account", "type": "fill", "id": "de10…", "address": "0x4ba4…", "ts": 1790486821609,
 "trade_id": "8545…", "intent_id": "18a3…", "contract_id": "3819…", "side": "buy", "role": "taker",
 "price": "0.0203", "amount": "1", "total_price": "0.0203", "tx_digest": "57k3…"}
```

- **Resting orders** get `fill` events. Only the order just placed gets an `order` event with its fill status; count a resting order's fills against its `total_amount` to know when it's done.
- **Unknown `type` values** may be added: ignore them.

## Starting and reconnecting

Nothing is replayed. To start, or after reconnecting:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe">
    Subscribe to the `account` channel for the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the current state">
    Read `GET /v1/accounts/{address}`, `/orders` and `/positions`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply events">
    Apply events from then on, dropping any whose `id` you've seen.
  </Step>
</Steps>
