## Tiles

A **tile** is one contract. It covers:
- an **underlying** (an instrument: BTC, ETH, SOL…);
- a **time window** `[start, end)`;
- a **price range** `[min_strike, max_strike)`.

A share pays **$1 if the underlying trades inside the range during the window**, and $0 otherwise. So share prices are between 0 and 1. The tile's id packs those five fields into a u256 ([contract ids](/signing#contract-ids)); `GET /v1/contracts/{id}` decodes it.

Listed today: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, HYPE and ZEC, all against USD.

## Grids

The exchange accepts any tile, but the market maker quotes, and liquidity gathers, on each instrument's **grids** (`GET /v1/instruments` → `grids`):

| Grid | Window | Price step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1m` | 60 s | the instrument's `price_step_usd` (BTC: $50) |
| `5m` | 300 s | the same step (BTC: $50) |
| `1h` | 3,600 s | 5 × that step (BTC: $250) |
| `1d` | 86,400 s | 10 × that step (BTC: $500) |

A grid tile starts on a multiple of the window (Unix seconds), and its range starts on a multiple of the price step. For BTC's 5m tile at time `t` and price `p`:

```text
start      = t - t % 300             end        = start + 300
min_strike = floor(p / 50) * 50      max_strike = min_strike + 50
```

Pack those into a [contract id](/signing#contract-ids), with the strikes in micro USD.

## Market hours

Each instrument has weekly market hours (`market_hours`, in `market_tz`). Crypto is always open (`""`), so every listed instrument today trades around the clock.

`GET /v1/instruments/{id}/sessions?from=&to=` turns market hours into absolute `[start_ms, end_ms]` sessions, up to 14 days at a time. A tile whose window doesn't overlap any session can't see a trade, so the exchange refuses it (`MARKET_CLOSED`).

## Is a tile tradable?

`GET /v1/contracts/{id}` gives the same answer `POST /v1/orders` enforces:

- **`status`:**
  - `upcoming`: the window hasn't started;
  - `active`: it's running;
  - `settling`: it has ended and is within the settlement grace period (`GET /v1/exchange` → `settlement_grace_seconds`);
  - `settled`: past that.
- **`tradable` and `reason`:** `TILE_ENDED` (the window has ended), `MARKET_CLOSED` (the window lies entirely in closed hours) or `UNKNOWN_INSTRUMENT`. Active tiles can still be traded.
- **`outcome`:** `yes` (touched) or `no`, once the tile has settled.

## Finding liquidity

`GET /v1/markets?instrument_id=&grid=` lists the tradable tiles that have resting orders, with each one's best bid and ask and its order count. It's the starting point for a liquidity provider. Stream any of them with the `book` or `top_of_book` [WebSocket channels](/api/websocket).

## The underlying's price

- `GET /v1/instruments/{id}/price` returns the last trade.
- The `underlying_trades` WebSocket channel streams every trade.

Prices come from the exchange's own feed of Coinbase spot trades. Settlement uses the same prices.
